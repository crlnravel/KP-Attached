# Research References

## Attach-SwiNet

The ATTACHED multimodal attachment-style workflow is documented alongside the
published Attach-SwiNet model. The paper combines non-verbal behavior signals
with ECR-RS responses for young-adult attachment-style classification.

> Maghfira, T. N., Basaruddin, T., Alfa Krisnadhi, A., & Redatin Retno Pudjiati,
> S. (2024). _Attach-SwiNet: Multimodal Attachment Style Classification Model
> Based on Non-Verbal Signals_. **IEEE Access, 12**, 79151–79165.
> https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2024.3397608

The machine-readable BibTeX entry is available in
[`references.bib`](./references.bib).
